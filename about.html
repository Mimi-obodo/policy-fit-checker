About Policy Fit Check

Policy Fit Check exists because insurance is confusing on purpose, or at least it can feel that way. Different providers, different fine print, different words for the same thing, and a hundred small conditions buried in a PDF nobody reads.

We built a company whose only job is to cut through that. We compare real policies from real providers and match you to the ones that actually fit your life: your age, your circumstances, your budget, the things you actually need covered.

We're not trying to sell you a policy. We're trying to help you understand one.

That's the difference. A comparison site shows you a list. We try to explain why one option on that list is right for you, and why the others aren't, in plain language, before you commit to anything.

A short story: why this exists

A friend of Mimi's, a nurse in her mid-twenties, once bought income protection because a colleague recommended it. She never read past the first page. Two years later, she needed to make a claim, and found out the policy excluded exactly the kind of injury she'd had, a detail that had been sitting in the small print the entire time.

Nothing about what she did was careless. She did what almost everyone does: she trusted that a sensible-sounding policy would sensibly cover her. It's the small print that let her down, not her judgement.

That's the gap Policy Fit Check is built to close. Not by making insurance simple to the point of leaving things out, but by making sure the fit is right before the signature happens, and that you actually know what you're signing.

Our Mission

To make insurance simpler, more transparent, and more personal, by helping people find the policy that actually fits who they are.

Our Vision

To become the place people trust when they need to make an insurance decision: somewhere you can compare your options, understand what you're looking at, and choose with confidence instead of guesswork.

Our Core Values

( 01 )

Customer First

Every decision starts with you, not with which provider pays us the most. We're thinking about your situation and what you'll actually need, not just what's easiest to sell today.

( 02 )

Transparency

We don't do guessing games. If a policy has a catch, we tell you about the catch. If something's a genuinely good deal, we tell you that too.

( 03 )

Trust

Insurance only works if you trust the people explaining it to you. We earn that by being honest even when honest is less convenient, and by never recommending something just because it's easier for us.

( 04 )

Personalisation

There's no such thing as the "best" policy for everyone. The right one depends on you: your age, your health, your budget, your life stage. We treat that as the whole point, not a detail.

( 05 )

Simplicity

Insurance is genuinely complicated. Our job is to make it feel simple without leaving out the parts you actually need to know. Simple, not simplified into meaninglessness.

( 06 )

Innovation

We use real technology, real data, and a genuinely useful set of tools to make finding insurance faster and clearer, while keeping a human, understandable explanation behind every recommendation.

( 07 )

Integrity

Our recommendations are guided by what's right for you, full stop. Not by hidden incentives, not by which provider is easiest to work with, not by anything except whether a policy is actually good for the person asking.

About the CEO

Here's the thing about insurance: almost nobody actually understands what they're buying. You fill in a form, you get a price, you pick the cheapest one, and you hope you never have to find out what it actually covers.

Mimi started Policy Fit Check because she kept watching people make that exact guess, over and over, with money they couldn't really afford to gamble.

"People should get the policy that's actually built for them, not just the one that's easiest to sell," she says. That's the whole idea, really. Everything else is just how we go about doing it.

As CEO, her job is to keep the company pointed at that one idea: build the technology, pick the partners, and make the calls that keep Policy Fit Check working for the customer first, every time, even when the easier business decision would be something else.

She's not trying to get you to buy insurance. She's trying to make sure that if you do, you actually know why you bought what you bought.

Before you choose any insurance, there's one question worth asking:

“Does this policy actually fit me?”
