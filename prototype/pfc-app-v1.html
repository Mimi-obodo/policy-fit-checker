Plain-language glossary
Premium
The amount you pay each month to keep the policy.
Excess (deductible)
You pay the first €X of any claim yourself; the insurer pays the rest.
Cover amount
The maximum the insurer will pay out on a claim.
Pre-existing condition
A health condition you already had before the policy started.
Pre-existing-condition exclusion
This policy won't cover a condition you already have.
Simplified underwriting
Fewer health questions before they accept you — easier to get, usually costs more.
Waiting period / moratorium
A condition you've had recently isn't covered at first; cover kicks in after a set time.
Indemnity vs pay-out
Indemnity reimburses actual costs; pay-out gives a fixed lump sum on a defined event.
