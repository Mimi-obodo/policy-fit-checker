How the five agents work

A small organisation, visible while it works. Click any agent to meet it, address it in the chat, or run a real match and watch the handoffs happen for real.

  1. Nadia Researcher

    Queries the live policy catalog at the moment you ask, filters it against your age band and life stage, and names the gaps honestly.

  2. Milo Designer

    Decides how the candidates should be compared, and scores every eligible policy against what actually matters for your profile.

  3. Priya Maker

    Builds your shortlist from the real catalog fields: name, provider, premium, coverage. Nothing is invented; if the data is missing, she says so.

  4. Sasha Communicator

    Writes the one-line reason under each policy in plain language, grounded in the actual numbers and the actual fine print.

  5. Callum Manager

    Reviews the handoffs, keeps the honest empty state visible when the catalog has nothing good, and synthesises the final recommendation.

This site runs on a static site with no backend. The agents' reasoning is deterministic and grounded in the live catalog, so the demo always works; the optional live-LLM variant is documented in the repo.
